Privacy

Fitness Planner by TrueHaven Studios – last updated October 2026.

The planner runs entirely in your browser. Everything you enter stays on your device. There is no account with us and no server of ours that receives your data – we cannot see it.

Apple Health

The optional “Fitness Sync” Apple Shortcut reads today's numbers from Apple Health on your iPhone and opens the planner with them. They go straight to the planner on your device – not to us.

Sign in with Google (optional)

If you sign in with Google, the planner saves a copy of your planner data in your own Google Drive, in a hidden app folder (scope drive.appdata). The planner can only see this one file – not your other Drive files. The data goes directly from your device to Google; we never receive it. You can sign out any time in Settings, and remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
