Privacy

Fitness Planner by TrueHaven Studios – last updated October 2026.

The planner runs entirely in your browser. Everything you enter is stored on your device. If you link your devices (optional), an end-to-end encrypted copy is kept on a sync server so your phone and computer stay the same. There is no account with us, and without sync nothing leaves your device.

Apple Health

The optional “Fitness Sync” Apple Shortcut reads today's numbers from Apple Health on your iPhone and opens the planner with them. They go straight to the planner on your device – not to us.

Device sync (optional)

If you link a second device in Settings, your planner data is locked (AES-256-GCM) on your device before it is sent. The sync server, a Cloudflare Worker with a small database, only ever stores scrambled data it cannot read, a random space ID, a version number and timestamps. The keys stay on your linked devices (and in the link or QR code you use to add a device). There is no account, no email and no password. We do not sell, share or use the data, and we cannot recover it for you. You can unlink a device or turn sync off and erase the server copy at any time in Settings › Your devices. Without sync, nothing leaves your device.
